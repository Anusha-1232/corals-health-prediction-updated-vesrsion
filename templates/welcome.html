Corals Health Prediction

Welcome to Coral Health Prediction System. Coral reefs are underwater ecosystems found in seas and oceans. They are made up of tiny marine organisms called corals. Coral reefs are very important for the environment because: They support nearly 25 percent of marine life, even though they cover a very small part of the ocean. They act as natural barriers that protect coastal areas from strong waves, storms, and tsunamis. They also support fishing, tourism, and marine biodiversity. If coral reefs are not present, it can lead to serious problems such as: loss of marine species, coastal erosion, increase in storm damage, and imbalance in the ocean ecosystem. Due to climate change, rising ocean temperatures, and pollution, coral reefs are getting damaged rapidly. To understand and predict coral health, we developed this Machine Learning based system. In this project, we use a dataset containing: year, water temperature, species richness, and coral region. We use Machine Learning algorithms such as: Random Forest, Decision Tree, and Linear Regression. These models analyze coral data and predict coral health conditions. The system classifies coral health into: Healthy, Moderate, and Poor categories. It also predicts coral density, health score, and future reef condition. The main purpose of this project is to help marine conservation, environmental monitoring, and climate change studies. This system can help scientists and researchers to take better decisions to protect ocean life. Now click the button to check coral health prediction.
